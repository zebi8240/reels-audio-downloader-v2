How to save reel audio

Three steps. The link has to be a public reel, post, or video.

1

Copy the link

In Instagram, open the reel, tap Share, then Copy link. On a computer, copy the address from the browser bar.

2

Paste it here

Use Paste, or drop the link in the box, then press Extract Audio. A pasted Instagram link starts on its own.

3

Choose MP3 or M4A

Preview the thumbnail, title, and creator. Play the audio, then download MP3 or M4A.

Questions

Is this free?

Yes. There is no account, no credit pack, and no limit built into the page.

How long does a reel take?

A public reel usually finishes in a few seconds. The first request can take longer if the server has been idle.

Should I download MP3 or M4A?

Choose MP3 when the file needs to play everywhere, including WhatsApp. Choose M4A when you want the smaller original-style file for editing on a computer.

Why will my M4A not play in WhatsApp?

Some chat apps only accept MP3. Download the MP3 and send that instead.

Will I see the song name?

If Instagram exposes a library track, the title and artist are shown. Original sounds show “Original audio” plus the creator.

Do I need to log in?

No. Private reels, close-friends posts, and stories that require a login cannot be fetched.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Use the browser you already have. No extra app is required.

Can I use the audio in my own videos?

Only if you have the right to use it. Songs and other people’s recordings are still theirs. This tool is for personal listening and for audio you are allowed to reuse.
